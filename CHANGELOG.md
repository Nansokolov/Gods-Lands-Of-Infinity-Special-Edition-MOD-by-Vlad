# Changelog - what the mod adds

Version 1.5.2. Most of this needs a new game.

## Controls and interface
- Hold Shift for 5x speed.
- Click pickup straight into the inventory; Ctrl+click for the original cursor pickup.
- The game log (`gamelog.htm`) stays on, which helps with bug reports.

## Fixes
- Unicorn Grove and elven arch bridges are wider to walk on.
- Steffene Arena round 2 (the Barbarian sisters) now happens.
- Deal'Anuwar's farewell no longer writes to the wrong dialogue.
- The elf ranger, An'aria and the Beastmaster no longer repeat their first meeting forever.
- A broken script line in the forest trap is fixed.

## Bounties and reputation
- Repeatable bounties: Radim (Petrovce), Captain Strezimir (Cursed Forest), Hedvika (Marshy Land), Bohdan (Raven Heights), Yarmila (Snowy Planes), Torvald (near the Snow Trolls' Camp).
- Bounty Clerk in Slavingrad.
- Hand-ins raise reputation, which unlocks shops and recipes.
- One enemy in three respawns in Sharkan's Dale, the Cursed Forest, Raven Heights and the Snowy Planes.

## Merchants
- Milena the Armourer and Oswin the Arcanist in Slavingrad: five stock tiers each, opened by quests with story and reputation requirements.
- Velimir the Mage in Petrovce sells basic crystals.
- Radoslava the Herbalist in Petrovce buys herbs, and three quests open her recipes and potions.
- Gatherers Milka, Oleg and Bozena sell cheap herbs that restock daily.
- Greater and Strong potions and rare recipes left the normal shops.

## Items and loot
- 14 crystals restored, including Sleep, Paralyze, Petrify and Blind, plus 27 pieces of gear that were disabled in the game.
- 51 items placed around the world, stronger further from the start.
- New drops: Cursed Elder Wolf, White Bear, Goron Matriarch, Troll Champion and Kordosh.

## Jobs
- Vivien and Ervin can use bows and magic staves.
- Zdenek the Bowyer (Slavingrad) teaches Archer 1-3.
- Havel the Hedge Mage (Raven Heights) teaches Mage 1-3.

## Hiring guild
- Bogumil in Slavingrad hires Militia Guards (1000 gold), Swordsmen (1500) and Guild Archers (1500) for 72 game hours.

## Bosses and side quests
- Grubnak the Exile (Raven Heights) wants Muzgrob and Sheg Ram dead.
- Asterion the Wanderer (Snowy Planes) wants Ruel Broix dead.
- Lord Radovan (Slavingrad) collects rare goods and pays with gold and his private armoury.

## Cursed Forest
- More enemy types: spiders, a spider queen, Rock Elements, Kordosh and Chrappa.

## The Undercroft (dungeon)
- The Warden in Slavingrad opens it for 2000 gold per entry.
- 16 monsters, weaker near the entrance and stronger further in. They return each time you enter.
- Boss Black Death pays 2500 gold once. Collect from the Warden or the Keeper at the entrance.
